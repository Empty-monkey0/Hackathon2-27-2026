Heat Score Formula

Weighted composite of 8 risk indicators

HeatScore = (
  0.22 × PrevGrade +
  0.18 × ConstructionProximity +
  0.15 × PriorViolations +
  0.12 × BuildingAge +
  0.10 × SubwayProximity +
  0.10 × BasementStorage +
  0.08 × MixedUseBuilding +
  0.05 × Complaints311_90d

Project Overview

The NYC Rat Reservoir Early Detection System is a predictive risk analytics platform designed to help the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (DOHMH) allocate limited inspection resources more effectively.

Instead of treating each restaurant as an isolated inspection unit, this system identifies Rat Reservoirs — clusters of high-risk blocks where building conditions, construction activity, and complaint patterns converge to create breeding grounds for persistent rodent colonies.

Data Pipeline

Three primary NYC Open Data sources joined via spatial proximity

-- Spatial Join: 311 complaints within 500ft of restaurant
SELECT r.camis, r.dba, r.zipcode, r.grade,
  COUNT(c.unique_key) AS complaints_90d,
  AVG(p.yearbuilt) AS avg_building_age
FROM dohmh_inspections r
LEFT JOIN complaints_311 c ON
  ST_DWithin(r.geom, c.geom, 152.4) -- 500 feet
  AND c.created_date >= r.inspection_date - INTERVAL '90 days'
LEFT JOIN pluto p ON r.bbl = p.bbl
GROUP BY r.camis, r.dba, r.zipcode, r.grade;
